Twelve months of your payouts are read from mainnet, and a transfer counts only if it comes from the network's own payout source. The limit is your median monthly flow in tokens at a 30-day median price, lowered for gaps and for volatility — each factor shown with its sign. Fewer than six paid months out of twelve: no limit, and the month you will get one.

A cap you set, and take back in one transaction.

You let the protocol draw on your HONEY account up to the debt plus interest — never more. The account stays yours. A single revoke ends it without asking anyone; the loan is then marked for manual repayment and says why.

The keeper sees the payout land, withholds the share, converts it and pays the debt in one operation. If the price has moved beyond tolerance, nothing is taken — the reward stays in your wallet, and the line says why.

Before you sign

What this build is, and what it is not.

  • It runs on devnet. No real money moves, and there is no mainnet deployment.
  • The HONEY it withholds is a devnet stand-in we mint, converted through a devnet vault, not a market. That proves the withholding — not that mainnet HONEY sells within tolerance at size, nor that Hivemapper's own payouts pass through unchanged.
  • Helium: your HNT history is shown, but no limit. The chain records when you claimed, not what the hotspot earned.
  • The loan is unsecured. Nothing is seized if you stop paying; overdue handling is not built yet.
  • One key signs every limit. Whoever holds it can issue any limit; it is visible on chain and can be rotated, not removed.
  • You can move a payout out before it is withheld. The protocol does not stop you.
  • The program has not been audited. The lenders' side of the pool is not open yet.
